Welcome!

This program lets you find and save recipes without the long life-stories included on most recipe sites. Its main feature is an allergen-filter system so you can easily find recipes that are safe for your specific dietary needs, whether that's a common allergy or something more unusual.

To get started, go to Import to save recipes from any link, then use Recipe Search to find and filter them. If you have questions, check the Information page.
